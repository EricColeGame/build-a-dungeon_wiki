## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I open the Build a Dungeon console during active sandbox sessions?
To open the **Build a Dungeon console**, head to the gameplay settings menu and verify that developer and sandbox tools are toggled on. On keyboard, press the tilde key (`~`), while on controllers, hold the View and Menu buttons simultaneously to reveal the diagnostic command line.

### Can using administrative console commands disable achievements?
In most game modes, entering commands that grant free currency, unlock high-tier rooms early, or bypass raid mechanics will flag the save file as a sandbox instance. This typically disables progression milestones and achievement tracking for that specific campaign.

### Does gamepad control support custom button mapping for room tools?
Yes. Modern controller profiles permit complete remapping for trigger placements, radial wheel categories, and camera zoom thresholds. Setting custom bindings helps players streamline repetitive tasks like wall excavation and corridor tiling.

### What should I do if the AI pathfinding glitches out inside deep corridors?
If creatures become immobile or cannot locate the dungeon core, activate the pathfinding overlay via the diagnostic command list (`show_navmesh 1`). Look for misaligned doorframes, unlinked stairs, or debris clipping into the corridor floor, and clear the affected tiles using the demolition tool.
