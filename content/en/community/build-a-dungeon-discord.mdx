## Dungeon Architecture: Community-Proven Layouts and Design Tips

Building a captivating dungeon requires balancing challenge with fairness. If your dungeon is too easy, invading raiders clear it without thought; if it is relentlessly unfair, players abandon it out of frustration. The **Build a Dungeon discord** features an active design subculture where builders debate architectural archetypes.

Player experience and community reports consistently highlight three dominant design archetypes in player-built gauntlets:

| Dungeon Archetype | Primary Mechanics | Core Defense Strength | Common Vulnerability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Choke-Point Gauntlet** | Narrow corridors, interlocking dart traps, heavy frontline mobs | High burst damage against careless squads | Vulnerable to long-range spellcasters and shield walls |
| **The Deceptive Labyrinth** | False doors, looping hallways, concealed pit hazards | Induces disorientation and splits party members | Can become trivial once an infiltrator maps the route |
| **The Resource Attrition Vault** | Wave spawners, lingering poison clouds, low health drop rates | Depletes party potions and cooldowns before the boss | Weak against agile speedrunners who bypass optional fights |

### Essential Construction Principles Shared by Builders

1.  **Avoid Unavoidable Damage:** Always provide subtle environmental tells (such as cracked floorboards or wall scorch marks) for instant-kill traps.
2.  **Respect the Mob Budget:** Avoid clustering all elite monsters into the entrance room; pace encounters gradually to build tension.
3.  **Encourage Exploration:** Reward observant explorers with hidden treasure alcoves behind secret walls to improve your dungeon's replay rating.
4.  **Test Alternate Routes:** Raiders often find ways to scale walls or bypass triggers. Have testers from the community attempt to break your intended progression path.

## Server Rules, Account Safety, and Verification Steps

Maintaining a secure account should always be your top priority when joining any online gaming hub. To keep discussion boards free of spammers and malicious actors, the community enforces strict onboarding standards based on standard [Discord Community Safety Guidelines](https://discord.com/safety) that every incoming member must follow.

When entering the **Build a Dungeon discord** for the first time, take several precautions to protect your personal information and gaming credentials:

*   **Never Share Account Credentials:** Legitimate developers and server staff will never ask for your passwords, two-factor authentication codes, or session tokens.
*   **Beware of Fake Tournament Invites:** Scammers frequently compromise community members and mass-message mutual servers with deceptive links promising free in-game currency or tournament prizes.
*   **Complete Official Verification Roles:** Most community servers require phone or captcha verification via recognized onboarding bots before unlocking text permissions.
*   **Disable Unsolicited Direct Messages:** If you receive strange direct messages from users sharing identical server badges, adjust your privacy settings to block direct messages from non-friends.

| Verification Issue | Probable Cause | Recommended Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Channels Appear Locked** | Unassigned onboarding role | Click the checkmark reaction or complete the captcha in the welcome channel |
| **Message Cooldown Active** | Slowmode enabled during peak hours | Wait out the timer; avoid spamming short, broken sentences |
| **Direct Messages Blocked** | Server privacy filter enabled | Add the trading partner as a friend before initiating trade messages |
| **Bot Commands Not Working** | Typing in standard chat channels | Move bot queries to designated `#bot-spam` or `#commands` channels |

Adhering to these steps ensures that your journey inside the community remains safe, enjoyable, and free from administrative penalties.

### Can I share my custom map codes in the Build a Dungeon discord?
