## Core Room Layouts and Architectural Flow

Constructing rooms haphazardly is the most common pitfall for newcomers. Testing structural patterns during the **Build a Dungeon demo** allows you to discover that layout geometry directly affects minion productivity and intruder survival rates.

Rooms should never be placed in a direct, straight line from the dungeon entrance to your Dungeon Core. Instead, adopt a serpentine or spiral configuration. This forces invader parties to spend maximum time navigating hazard-filled corridors while giving your sentries time to rally and ambush them from flanking angles.

| Room Name | Primary Function | Optimal Adjacency | Hazard Level to Core |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dungeon Core** | Lifeline of your domain; if destroyed, the run ends | Deepest chamber, Vault, Guard Sanctum | Critical (Protect at all costs) |
| **Barracks** | Resting quarters and healing zone for combat minions | Mess Hall, Armory | Low (Keep behind active defense lines) |
| **Mess Hall** | Sustenance hub; keeps minion unrest low | Storage Cellar, Barracks | Low (Central interior access) |
| **Gold Vault** | Stores mined and looted currency | Dungeon Core, Treasury Guardposts | High (Primary target for thieving heroes) |
| **Training Arena** | Levels up minion combat proficiencies | Barracks, Forward Chokepoints | Moderate (Buffer zone between waves) |

### The "Deadly Switchback" Design

A proven layout favored by veteran dungeon builders is the serpentine switchback. By carving zigzag corridors flanked by reinforced walls, you force invading warriors to walk past multiple trap zones. 

Make sure to place your Barracks adjacent to a secondary side corridor rather than the main pathway. This prevents exhausted or sleeping minions from being ambushed before they can equip their gear and organize a defense.

### Managing Adjacency Penalties

Some rooms produce noise, heat, or structural instability that reduces the productivity of neighboring spaces:
- Keep noisy Training Arenas away from sleeping Barracks to avoid rest interruptions.
- Place Mess Halls within 15 tiles of working zones so workers waste minimal time traveling during break shifts.
- Insulate Gold Vaults with heavy doors and reinforced stonework to delay rogues who slip past forward perimeter traps.

## Trap Synergy and Defense Optimization

Traps form the backbone of security across your early runs in the **Build a Dungeon demo**, functioning as force multipliers against overwhelming hero numbers. A single spike trap rarely kills a high-level knight, but layering complementary hazards turns corridors into automated slaughterhouses.

The most effective trap lines utilize crowd control (CC) to hold heroes inside continuous damage zones. When an adventurer triggers a slowing mechanism, environmental damage effects like falling boulders or poison darts have ample time to cycle through their cooldowns.

| Trap Combination | Mechanism Trigger | Target Hero Class | Resource Efficiency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tar Pit + Fire Grate** | Slowed movement ignites into sustained burn damage | Armored Warriors, Paladins | High (Low gold cost, high damage return) |
| **Spring Floor + Pit Fall** | Displaces targets backward into earlier hazard tiles | Rogue Scouts, Speedrunners | Exceptional (Resets hero pathing progress) |
| **Poison Darts + Decoy Totem**| Ranged affliction while heroes attack dummy target | Spellcasters, Ranged Clerics | Moderate (Requires regular totem repair) |
| **Crushing Wall + Barricade** | Compresses bottlenecks when doorways are blocked | Clustered raiding parties | High (Devastating burst against tight packs) |

### Exploiting Adventurer AI Behavior

Adventurers calculate pathing using the path of least physical resistance toward loot or your core. If you place a heavy iron door at one hallway and leave an open, trap-laden detour beside it, heroes will almost always take the open route. 

Player experience indicates that leaving tempting low-tier treasure chests as bait in the middle of pressure-plate matrices is an exceptional way to eliminate greedy rogue scouts before they can disarm your critical defenses. Always inspect adventurer stats on the [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com) or in-game dossier to customize your trap triggers accordingly.

## Minion Management and Resource Economy

Constructing lethal rooms means nothing if your workforce revolts due to starvation or wage theft. Keeping your creatures satisfied remains a balancing act within the **Build a Dungeon demo**, where economic margins leave little room for waste.

Your economy relies on three fundamental pillars:
1. **Raw Gold**: Mined from mineral veins or stripped from fallen heroes.
2. **Food Rations**: Cultivated in subterranean fungal farms or butchered from subterranean fauna.
3. **Mana Essence**: Siphoned from leyline fractures to fuel magical traps and summon higher-tier monsters.

| Minion Archetype | Daily Upkeep | Combat Specialty | Primary Vulnerability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Goblin Digger** | 1 Food, 1 Gold | Tunnel excavation, minor repairs | Very frail; panics easily under attack |
| **Orc Brute** | 3 Food, 4 Gold | Frontline tanking, knockback strikes | Weak against magical projectile attacks |
| **Dark Sorcerer** | 2 Food, 5 Gold, 1 Mana | Long-range area curses, magic defense | Low physical armor; fragile in melee |
| **Skeleton Sentry**| 1 Mana (No Food/Gold) | Static guard duty, fear immunity | Bludgeoning weapons deal double damage |

To preserve your budget, avoid recruiting high-cost Orcs or Sorcerers until your basic farm-and-vault loop produces a steady surplus. Skeletons serve as outstanding low-maintenance garrison units early on because they require no food or sleep, making them ideal sentries for forward border outposts.

## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Surviving Your First Hero Waves

Conquering your first full run requires structured planning across distinct escalation phases. Based on early player experience and community reports, beating the final wave of the **Build a Dungeon demo** demands discipline over rapid, uncontrolled expansion.

### Phase 1: Excavation and Containment (Days 1–3)
- Carve out a 5x5 Central Core chamber and seal all perimeter access except for a single entry corridor.
- Establish a starter 3x3 Mess Hall and small Barracks capable of supporting 4 Goblins and 2 Orcs.
- Mine local gold veins while preserving natural granite walls as natural barrier choke points.

### Phase 2: Fortification and Funneling (Days 4–7)
- Build your first winding switchback leading from the surface entry to your interior gates.
- Install alternating Tar Pit and Spike Traps along the bottleneck corridor.
- Position a Training Arena adjacent to the chokepoint so your frontline fighters level up their defense attributes during downtime.

### Phase 3: The Elite Incursion (Days 8–10)
- The demo culminates in a coordinated strike featuring armored crusaders accompanied by ranged spell support.
- Split your defender formations: deploy Orc Brutes to lock warriors in chokepoints while dispatching fast rogue or goblin skirmishers through flank tunnels to eliminate fragile enemy healers.
- Manually trigger wall traps when enemy clusters bottleneck around heavy iron barricades to clear waves simultaneously.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Build a Dungeon Demo

### How long does a full playthrough of the Build a Dungeon demo take?
A standard run through the current demo build takes between 45 and 90 minutes, depending on how methodically you design your layouts and whether you survive the escalating hero waves on your first attempt.

### Can trap setups carry over if I redesign a hallway?
Yes. Deconstructing traps refunds a portion of your raw building materials, allowing you to reposition defensive networks as you expand deeper into lower subterranean strata.

### What should I do if adventurers bypass my defenses?
If fast heroes slip through your primary corridor, trigger emergency lockdown doors to seal them into side chambers. While they spend time battering down reinforced wood or iron gates, redeploy your garrison units to flank them from behind.

### Will save files from the Build a Dungeon demo transfer to the full release?
Developer notes and community reports suggest that while foundational achievements and unlocks may carry over, major structural updates to map generation at full launch will likely require starting fresh dungeons to access all new features properly.
