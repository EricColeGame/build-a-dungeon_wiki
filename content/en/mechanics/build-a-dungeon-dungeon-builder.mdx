## Advanced Layout Blueprinting: The Funnel Gauntlet Strategy

To achieve consistent defensive victories in the **Build a Dungeon dungeon builder**, top architects rely on the Funnel Gauntlet strategy. This design channels invaders through narrow entry bottlenecks into wide "kill amphitheaters" where player-controlled hazards and minion crossfire work in perfect harmony.

### Step 1: The Winding Vestibule
Construct an initial hallway that snakes across the grid rather than proceeding in a straight line. Straight hallways allow enemy rangers to fire freely from maximum range. Winding turns force adventurers to round corners directly into point-blank trap triggers and line-of-sight ambushes.

### Step 2: The Decoy Cache
Position a small, moderately defended side-vault near the dungeon entrance containing low-tier loot. Wandering scouts and rogue units will break off from the main party to loot this secondary objective, separating their damage dealers from their defensive tanks.

### Step 3: The Elevated Firing Gallery
Build balconies along your primary kill chamber. Stationing ranged minions like archers or warlocks on elevated tiles prevents enemy warriors from engaging them in melee combat, forcing the invaders to endure steady ranged bombardment while dealing with frontline blockers below.

### Step 4: The Pressure Valve Exit
Always incorporate a secondary access corridor accessible only to your own dungeon units via locked reinforced blast doors. This allows your patrol units to quickly reinforce besieged rooms or retreat to recover health without providing intruders an easy shortcut to your core.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most common early-game mistake in the Build a Dungeon dungeon builder?
The most frequent early-game mistake is over-excavating before establishing a stable resource economy. Carving out massive rooms drains your starting gold reserves and increases corridor traversal times, leaving your under-leveled minions spread too thin to defend incoming raids effectively.

### How does the Build a Dungeon dungeon builder calculate adventurer target priority?
Adventurers evaluate target priority using an incentive weight matrix. They weigh room proximity against perceived reward value, prioritizing glowing treasure chests, unprotected magical shrines, and exposed resource stockpiles over well-guarded barracks, unless attacked by aggressive sentinels along the path.

### Can traps trigger friendly fire damage on allied dungeon minions?
In most standard configurations, basic mechanical traps do not trigger when friendly defenders walk across them. However, widespread environmental hazards—such as oil fires, poison gas clouds, and boulder slides—deal unmitigated area damage to every unit caught within their blast radius, including your own monsters.

### How do I increase structural integrity against siege-specialist adventurers?
To counter siege-oriented classes that smash through walls to bypass traps, replace standard stone corridors with Reinforced Masonry or Volcanic Basalt. Additionally, placing heavy Gargoyle Turrets or runic wards on adjoining walls drastically reduces structural damage from pickaxes and explosive charges.
