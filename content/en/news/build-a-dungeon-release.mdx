## Core Systems: Lair Excavation, Room Types, and Defense Networks

At its heart, dungeon creation relies on a delicate balance between defensive lethality and operational efficiency. You cannot simply build miles of spiky corridors; your minions need barracks to rest, pantries for nourishment, and research chambers to discover higher-tier traps. When evaluating what tools will be available upon the **Build a Dungeon release**, the modular room network stands out as the game's structural backbone.

Rooms must be connected via functional corridors that do not compromise the defensive integrity of your fortress. If your barracks are positioned too far from the front entrance, your frontline guards may arrive long after intruders have raided your vault.

| Room Archetype | Primary Function | Operational Cost | Strategic Synergy |

## Designing Lethal Gauntlets: Trap Placement and Synergies

A dungeon without traps is merely an underground warehouse waiting to be looted. Trapping mechanics in modern builders focus heavily on physics interactions, status afflictions, and environmental hazards that trigger sequentially. 

The secret to a brutal defense is avoiding isolated, single-activation traps. Instead, skilled architects link pressure plates, tripwires, and delayed triggers into cascading death traps that eliminate entire hero parties simultaneously.

```
[Entry Hall] -> (Slowing Slime Pit) -> (Wall Dart Volley) -> [Boulder Launch Mechanism]
                       |                                                |
                       +---> (Hero Movement Slowed by 50%)              +---> (Crushing Physical Damage)
```

To optimize your traps upon launch, consider these four battle-tested combinations:

*   **The Tar-and-Blaze Corridor:** Coat oncoming intruders in flammable residue via oil dispensers, followed by a ceiling-mounted flame jet.
*   **The Kinetic Pitfall:** Use spring-loaded floorboards to launch armored warriors into deep chasms lined with wall-mounted spikes.
*   **The Decoy Treasure Room:** Place an illuminated, unlocked chest at the end of a corridor, baiting rogue classes directly into explosive runic runes.
*   **The Drowning Channel:** Funnel high-defense paladins into a recessed basin and release floodgates to bypass heavy plate armor through environmental drowning.

Mastering early-game choke points before the **Build a Dungeon release** goes live gives new architects an immediate defensive edge over incoming waves of adventurers.

## Progression Curves: Managing Minion Morale and Resources

A formidable dungeon is only as reliable as the creatures defending it. Minion AI in contemporary dungeon titles is no longer completely subservient; your denizens have specific dietary needs, rest cycles, salary demands, and morale meters. Neglecting these requirements can lead to work strikes, infighting, or minions deserting your dungeon entirely.

### Balancing the Internal Economy

Your economy rests upon three essential pillars:

1.  **Gold and Mineral Extraction:** Excavate veins to pay weekly mercenary wages and forge traps.
2.  **Mana Collection:** Tap into ley line nodes to power magical defenses and fuel summoning circles.
3.  **Provisions and Sustenance:** Maintain mushroom farms, hunting pens, and breweries to keep minion combat strength high.

| Minion Type | Primary Role | Preferred Sustenance | Morale Risk Factor |

| **Undead Knight** | Unflinching gatekeeper | Souls of vanquished heroes | Immune to morale drops, slow moving |

Managing these personalities creates an intriguing juggling act. Preparing your operational workflow in anticipation of the **Build a Dungeon release** involves optimizing layouts so that your workers spend more time producing and defending rather than commuting between living quarters.
