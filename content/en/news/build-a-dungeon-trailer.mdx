## Essential Strategies for Aspiring Dungeon Architects

Mastering dungeon construction requires discipline, careful budgeting, and forward planning. While the game provides ample creative freedom, following tested architectural principles prevents catastrophic collapses during mid-game hero invasions.

*   **Establish Tiered Perimeters:** Never connect your outer gate directly to valuable infrastructure. Build a buffer zone of winding hallways designed exclusively to soften up intruders with low-cost traps.
*   **Balance Your Ledger Early:** Over-recruiting heavy combat units before establishing automated gold mines causes rapid financial ruin. Keep your standing army lean during the initial excavation phases.
*   **Group Complementary Minions:** Certain creature types harbor innate rivalries. Avoid placing demon quarters adjacent to undead tombs to prevent costly infighting among your defenders.
*   **Leverage Elevation and Choke Points:** Force invaders to fight uphill or funnel through single-tile doorways where area-of-effect spells inflict maximum damage on clumped formations.
*   **Maintain Reserve Patrols:** Keep a small detachment of agile units unassigned to specific rooms so they can quickly reinforce breached sectors when traps fail.

By implementing these structural fundamentals, you can build a self-sustaining underground fortress that can weather unexpected difficulty spikes and prolonged siege waves.
