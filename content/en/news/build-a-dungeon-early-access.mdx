## Designing the Ultimate Gauntlet: Traps, Minions, and Layouts

A successful dungeon does not rely on brute force alone; it funnels heroes into deceptive kill zones where traps and minions work in tandem. Early game invaders usually march straight toward the nearest treasure chest or your dungeon core. You can exploit this predictable pathing by building zigzag corridors loaded with layered hazards.

In the current **Build a Dungeon early access** meta, passive slowing traps paired with sustained area-of-effect damage provide the highest return on investment. If you position heavy brawlers immediately behind an activated floor trigger, invading champions are forced to fight while suffering continuous elemental damage.

| Trap Classification | Resource Cost | Trigger Type | Best Paired Minion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spike Pit** | Low (Iron & Wood) | Pressure Plate | Ranged Archers / Crossbow Goblins |
| **Poison Dart Wall** | Moderate (Darts & Stone) | Line-of-Sight Sensor | High-HP Tanks (Trolls, Golems) |
| **Rolling Boulder** | Moderate (Granite Block) | Manual Pull / Tripwire | Fast Harassers (Imps, Bats) |
| **Frost Rune** | High (Mana & Runestones) | Proximity Arcane | Heavy Spellcasters / Necromancers |
| **Pitfall Chute** | High (Mechanical Gears) | Hidden Floor Trap | Isolated Ambush Squads |

According to player experience shared during testing, relying solely on high-damage single-target traps often allows fast rogue invaders to slip through undetected. Always incorporate redundancy: place sensory traps at the door, slowing mechanisms in the center, and a defensive garrison at the exit.

## Essential Tips for Getting Started on Day One

Starting a new underground domain can feel overwhelming when resources are tight and adventuring parties begin knocking on your door. Setting up an orderly expansion plan ensures you never run out of building materials or defensive units during early invasion waves.

Follow these proven steps to establish a self-sustaining dungeon footprint right from day one:

1. **Protect Your Gold Veins**: Carve straight toward mineral deposits first. Never build luxury rooms until your initial gold vault is functional.
2. **Control Ingress Points**: Limit external entry doors to a single fortified threshold. Having multiple unguarded entrances forces you to split your defending forces thin.
3. **Establish a Clean Choke Point**: Ensure all tunnels leading to your sanctum converge into a single hallway lined with basic floor spikes.
4. **Maintain Minion Happiness**: Feed and pay your forces before expanding territory. Unhappy minions cause disruptive strikes and decrease overall room productivity.
5. **Research Arcane Upgrades**: Prioritize automated traps early so your living guards do not take unnecessary casualties during minor skirmishes.

| Phase of Play | Primary Objective | Target Gold Reserve | Recommended Garrison |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Days 1–5** | Secure ore, build sleeping quarters | 500–1,000 Gold | 3–5 Basic Goblins / Imps |
| **Days 6–15** | Deploy first trap corridor, expand larder | 2,500–5,000 Gold | 8–10 Mixed Units (Melee + Ranged) |
| **Days 16–30** | Unlock magic rooms, reinforce dungeon core | 10,000+ Gold | 15+ Advanced Minions & Mini-Boss |

Sticking to these developmental benchmarks prevents overexpansion while keeping your treasury resilient against economic dips.
